# Decision critique skill

## Objective
Evaluate whether a decision is being made on evidence or on momentum.

## Inputs
- strategy briefs
- launch plans
- product decisions

## Process
1. Identify the decision in question
2. Extract the assumptions that support it
3. Identify what evidence exists versus what is missing
4. Turn missing evidence into prioritized experiments

## Expected output
- decision audit
- evidence map
- missing-data list
- recommended test plan

## Review criteria
- Does the recommendation separate desire from evidence?
- Is the team asked to test the real uncertainty rather than debate preferences?
- Is the output actionable and time-bound?
