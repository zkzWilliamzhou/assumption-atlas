# Contradiction scan skill

## Objective
Surface conflicts inside the same plan or document before the team commits to execution.

## Inputs
- documents with roadmap, product, or launch plans
- strategic notes
- research summaries

## Process
1. Look for contrast markers such as "however", "but", "despite", "on the other hand"
2. Compare adjacent statements for opposing signals
3. Highlight trade-offs and unresolved tension
4. Rank contradictions by impact and fixability

## Expected output
- contradiction summary
- affected decision areas
- suggested experiment or decision policy

## Review criteria
- Is the contradiction real, or just a rhetorical phrase?
- Is the tension central to the decision?
- Does the conclusion point toward a test, not a debate?
