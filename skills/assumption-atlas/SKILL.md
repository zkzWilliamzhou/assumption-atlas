# Assumption Atlas skill

## Objective
Find the assumptions and beliefs that are driving a plan or product idea without direct validation.

## Inputs
- product briefs
- roadmap notes
- idea memos
- strategy documents
- transcripts

## Process
1. Scan for explicit belief statements such as "we assume", "we believe", "users want", "must ship"
2. Extract those claims from context and label them as assumptions
3. Rank assumptions by business impact and uncertainty
4. Convert the highest-risk assumptions into validation experiments

## Expected output
- a list of assumptions
- a short rationale for each
- a confidence or risk score
- a set of next experiments

## Review criteria
- Are the assumptions genuinely unproven?
- Are the claims tied to evidence, or merely preferred belief?
- Does the experiment reduce uncertainty rather than create more noise?
